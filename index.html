The Week 02 Formula

One message. Five deliberate moves.

Great creative does more than look attractive. It earns attention, communicates value and gives the audience a reason to act.

01 · HOOKStop the scroll
02 · MESSAGEMake it clear
03 · BENEFITShow the value
04 · EMOTIONCreate connection
05 · ACTIONGuide the next step
01 — 07 · Copywriting

Build the message before the design.

Learn to write with clarity, consistency and customer value at the centre.

01 / HEADLINES & HOOKS

Win the first second.

A headline makes people stop. Use curiosity, problem awareness, desire, surprise, emotion or urgency.

“Why are your ads getting clicks but no customers?”
02 / FEATURES VS BENEFITS

Translate product into value.

FEATURELightweight material
BENEFITRun comfortably for longer

Customers care about what a feature helps them achieve.

03 / TRANSFORMATION

Show the before and after.

Describe the customer's current state, your solution and their desired state.

CURRENT STATE → SOLUTION → DESIRED STATE
04 / BRAND VOICE

Sound recognisable.

Choose a consistent personality: professional, friendly, premium, bold, educational, playful or trustworthy.

05 / VOICE VS TONE

Keep the personality. Adapt the moment.

Voice is who the brand is. Tone is how it speaks in a particular situation. A complaint needs a calm, helpful and respectful tone.

06 / CALL TO ACTION

Make the next step obvious.

Use ACTION + VALUE. Replace “Download” with “Download your free marketing checklist”.

07 / EDITING & REWRITING

Make every word work.

WRITE → REVIEW → EDIT → SIMPLIFY → STRENGTHEN. Remove clutter, clarify the benefit and make the CTA unmistakable.

08 — 12 · Creative Skills

Design so the message gets understood.

Visual communication guides attention and makes information easier to process.

08 / VISUAL HIERARCHY

Control the order of attention.

Guide the viewer: headline → main visual → supporting information → CTA / brand.

09 / COMPOSITION

Give every element a place.

Use balance, alignment, spacing, proportion, contrast, white space and a clear focal point.

10 / COLOUR PSYCHOLOGY

Use colour with context.

Red: energy · Blue: trust · Green: growth · Yellow: optimism · Purple: creativity · Black: sophistication · White: simplicity. These are guides, not fixed rules.

11 / TYPOGRAPHY

Make words easy to read.

Consider font choice, size, weight, line spacing, letter spacing and alignment. Create a clear headline, subheadline, body and CTA hierarchy.

12 / ACCESSIBILITY

Design for more people.

Use readable sizes, strong contrast, simple language, video captions and clear layouts. Never communicate important information through colour alone.

THE CONNECTION

Copy + creative = communication.

HOOK → MESSAGE → BENEFIT → VISUAL → CTA. Strategy gives every creative element a purpose.

Hands-on learning

Five tasks. One real portfolio.

Use the same business you selected in Week 01. Build manually, explain your decisions and keep your work organised.

Write 15 headlines

Explore problem, benefit, curiosity, question, transformation, emotion and urgency angles.

Select your strongest headline

Score it for clarity, relevance, attention, benefit and audience fit. Explain your choice.

Create 3 social media creatives

Make one educational, one promotional and one problem → solution creative. Include headline, visual, supporting copy, CTA and branding.

Storyboard a 15–30 sec video

0–3s hook · 3–8s problem · 8–20s solution · 20–25s benefit · 25–30s CTA.

Critique 3 existing creatives

Evaluate copy, hierarchy, composition, colour, typography and accessibility. State what you would improve.
